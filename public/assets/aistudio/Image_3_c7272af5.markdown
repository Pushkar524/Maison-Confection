## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, celebratory, and tactile luxury aesthetic. Designed for an artisanal gift-box and bespoke event stationery atelier, the interface merges high-fashion editorial craftsmanship with joyous, uninhibited warmth. The target audience seeks thoughtful indulgence—they value handcrafted details, thoughtful unboxing rituals, and tailored sentimentality without stuffiness or sterile minimalism.

The visual direction combines **Contemporary Tactile Luxury** with **Playful Neoclassicism**:
- **Soft Pillowy Volumes:** Surfaces possess deep comfort, mimicking plush handmade cardstock, ribbons, and cushioned box linings.
- **Editorial Chic:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke heritage Parisian print ateliers and bespoke invitations, anchored by clean, legible, rounded body typography.
- **Curated Exuberance:** A neutral, warm cream foundation lets vibrant apricot, punchy coral, sunshine yellow, and dreamy lilac burst forth in controlled, high-delight moments (stamps, ribbons, badges, micro-stickers, and festive confetti accents).

## Colors

The palette is layered intentionally around warmth, celebration, and romantic charm:

- **Foundation (Neutrals):**
  - Alabaster Cream (`#FDF8F3`): Canvas background and expansive negative space.
  - Biscuit Beige (`#F6EFE9`): Pillowy card fills, modular panels, and layered sheets.
  - Almond Oat (`#ECE2D8`): Structural dividers, soft inset borders, and unselected control rings.
  - Espresso Ink (`#2D2321`): High-legibility text baseline with warm, organic undertones rather than harsh pure black.
  - Cocoa Muted (`#7A6A65`): Captions, secondary timestamps, and non-active labels.

- **Romantic Core (Pinks):**
  - Petal Rose (`#F7CAD0` / `#F4ACB7`): Delicate hover states, background highlights, and gift wrap previews.
  - Deep Blush (`#FF8FAB`): Interactive secondary toggles, badges, and floral indicators.

- **Vibrant Accents:**
  - Punchy Coral (`#FF5A5F` - Primary): Direct call-to-actions, cart counts, and joyful celebration triggers.
  - Lilac Dusk (`#A29BFE` - Secondary): Bespoke customization flows, ribbon selectors, and artisanal tags.
  - Warm Mandarin (`#FF9F1C` - Tertiary): Surprise offers, festive badges, and event-tier distinctions.
  - Sunshine Mimosa (`#FFE15D`): Sparkle iconography, rating stars, and celebratory confetti accents.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional tension between editorial grace and approachable, rounded friendliness:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Utilized for brand statements, collection titles, product naming, and bespoke suite narratives. It balances high romance with classic luxury typography. Italic variations are encouraged for decorative emphasis (e.g., *Personalized* with Care).
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, geometric, and softly radiused. Delivers clear legibility across long product descriptions, builder steps, personalization forms, and pricing matrices.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Never set `Playfair Display` in all-caps or at sizes under 24px. Use `Plus Jakarta Sans` for UI labels, pricing tags, badge copy, and input fields to maintain crisp visual order.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a generous, airy **Fluid Grid System** anchored by distinct rhythm and pillowed modular boundaries:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a minimum 48px (`3rem`) page margin. Max-width content boundary capped at 1360px for immersive yet legible storytelling.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px page margins. Bespoke package builders transition from multi-column splits into layered split-screen configurations.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) safe edge margins. Horizontal carousels snap along card boundaries, revealing peek-previews of secondary gift add-ons.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8-point base scale with generous vertical separation between narrative editorial blocks (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) to preserve an unhurried, luxury boutique ambiance.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile, warm, and dimensional—suggesting physical stationery, thick cotton rag papers, and cushioned gift box lids:

- **Surface Tonal Stacking:** The base background is Alabaster Cream (`#FDF8F3`). Floating modules sit on Biscuit Beige (`#F6EFE9`) or pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with whisper-thin, warm borders (`#ECE2D8`).
- **Warm Diffused Shadows:** Never use cold or neutral gray drop shadows. All shadows incorporate a warm terracotta or rose tint (`rgba(122, 60, 40, 0.06)` to `rgba(122, 60, 40, 0.12)`).
  - *Resting Card:* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(122, 60, 40, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(122, 60, 40, 0.03)`
  - *Floating/Hover State:* `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(122, 60, 40, 0.10), 0 6px 12px -2px rgba(122, 60, 40, 0.04)`
  - *Pillowy Dialog/Modal:* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(80, 30, 20, 0.15)`
- **Embossing & Debossing:** Subtle inner glow highlights (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) on buttons and card surfaces to emulate pressed letterpress paper goods.

## Shapes

The shape system is friendly, soft, and tailored:

- **Standard Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px):** Emulates cushioned gift boxes and heavyweight rounded-corner card decks.
- **Micro UI Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Swatches, input fields, and stepper controls maintain rounded edges without collapsing into circles.
- **Interactive Badges & Pills (`full-rounded` / 9999px):** Status badges, category tabs, filter tags, and primary action buttons adopt full pill treatments to reinforce celebratory playfulness.
- **Specialty Shapes:** Postage-stamp scalloped edges and ribbon pennants are permitted on promotional tags and card customization preview frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Celebratory CTA):** Punchy Coral (`#FF5A5F`) fill, pure white text, pill-shaped (`9999px`). Features a subtle top inner-bevel light reflection and a tinted drop shadow. On hover, scales slightly (`1.02x`) with an expanded warm glow.
- **Secondary (Artisanal Customizer):** Biscuit Beige (`#F6EFE9`) background with Lilac Dusk (`#A29BFE`) or Deep Espresso text and a 1.5px border in Petal Rose (`#F4ACB7`).
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Transparent fill, underlined serif or bold rounded sans with coral arrow icon on hover.

### Cards & Packaging Tiles
- **Product Card:** Pillowy, high-radius background (`#FFFFFF` or `#F6EFE9`) wrapped in a 1px border (`#ECE2D8`). Contains image stage with soft pastel backdrop (`#F7CAD0` or `#FDF8F3`), favorite heart toggle, and animated "Quick Personalize" overlay.
- **Bespoke Step Tile:** Numbered pill counter, tactile press interaction on selection, ring outline shifts from `#ECE2D8` to `#FF5A5F` with a soft 4px coral outer shadow ring.

### Badges & Joyful Tags
- **Vibe & Tag Pills:** Full pill-shaped tags featuring saturated accent backgrounds with contrasting dark or white text:
  - *Best Seller / Festive:* Punchy Coral (`#FF5A5F`) with white text.
  - *Custom Monogram:* Lilac Dusk (`#A29BFE`) with espresso text.
  - *Limited Edition:* Warm Mandarin (`#FF9F1C`) with white text.
  - *Handcrafted:* Sunshine Mimosa (`#FFE15D`) with dark espresso text.

### Inputs & Personalization Controls
- **Text & Monogram Fields:** 48px height, rounded corners (`0.75rem`), warm cream fill (`#FFFFFF`), with an outline border in `#ECE2D8`. Focus state transitions border to `#FF5A5F` accompanied by an ambient warm coral ring (`rgba(255, 90, 95, 0.2)`).
- **Font & Foil Pickers:** Rounded square swatches with metallic foil gradient previews (Gold, Rose Gold, Blind Deboss) enclosed in smooth inset borders.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Radio Buttons:** Custom rounded pill containers. Selected state fills with `#FF5A5F` and features an inner white dot; alternative layout features selectable rounded swatch cards that glow when active.
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth rounded squares (`6px` radius) in Espresso or Coral with a clean white checkmark on active state.

### Lists & Add-on Drawers
- **Curated Box Item List:** Divided by whisper-soft borders (`#ECE2D8`). Includes thumbnail with rounded corners (`8px`), quantity pill steppers with tactile `+` / `-` press feedback, and celebratory micro-animations when an item is added to the gift box.
